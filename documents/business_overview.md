# Business Overview (Synthetic Demo Data)

The company sells electronics and furniture across North, South, East, and West regions.
The sales database contains sample orders from January through September 2026.
Revenue is recorded in USD. Cost is the direct product cost and excludes overhead.
Gross profit for this demo can be calculated as revenue minus cost.
The Enterprise customer segment primarily purchases laptops and other high-value devices.
The SMB segment purchases a mix of furniture and electronics. Consumer orders include phones
and furniture. These records are synthetic and intended only for demonstrating retrieval.
